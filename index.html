Annexure F

The admission cycle, as the desk experiences it

Stage-by-stage, this is where a counsellor's time actually goes and where automation would remove the most work. Exact dates shift every year with the exam calendar — treat the sequence as fixed and the calendar as something to confirm annually at source.

Stage 01Enquiry

Parent or student makes first contact — usually WhatsApp or a phone call, rarely email. Speed of first reply decides most of it.

Stage 02Profile & eligibility

Board, stream, marks, entrance attempt, category, domicile state, budget ceiling. Everything downstream depends on getting this right once.

Stage 03Shortlist & fee comparison

Course and college options laid against total cost. This is the conversation the family remembers, and the desk's current paper form.

Stage 04Registration & forms

Entrance registration, corrections window, fee payment, admit card. Every deadline here is unforgiving.

Stage 05Result & rank

Score, rank, percentile, category rank. The shortlist gets rebuilt against reality.

Stage 06Choice filling & locking

The highest-skill moment on the desk. Order of preferences decides the seat. Unlocked choices are the most common avoidable loss.

Stage 07Allotment & rounds

Round-wise results, freeze or float or slide, upgrade decisions, mop-up and spot rounds.

Stage 08Reporting & verification

Physical reporting, original document check, fee payment. One missing certificate loses a confirmed seat.

Stage 09Post-admission

Hostel, loan, scholarship, migration certificate. Where referrals are earned.

Annexure H

Desk checklists

The two document lists the desk repeats hundreds of times a season. Requirements vary by state, category and institution — confirm against the specific institution's prospectus each time.

Domestic admission — student file

  • Class 10 marksheet and passing certificate
  • Class 12 marksheet and passing certificate
  • Entrance scorecard and rank letter
  • Seat allotment letter
  • Transfer certificate from the last school
  • Migration certificate, where the board requires it
  • Domicile or residence certificate
  • Category certificate, with validity checked
  • Non-creamy-layer certificate for OBC, current year
  • EWS certificate, current year
  • Income certificate
  • Disability certificate, where applicable
  • Aadhaar and one photo ID
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Medical fitness certificate
  • Gap certificate or affidavit, if there is a break in study
  • Fee payment receipts, kept in original

Study abroad — student file

  • Passport, with validity checked against course end
  • Full academic transcripts and degree certificates
  • English test result — IELTS, TOEFL, PTE or Duolingo, per university
  • Statement of purpose
  • Letters of recommendation
  • CV
  • Proof of funds, and a blocked account where the country requires one
  • Credential evaluation, such as the APS certificate for Germany
  • University offer or admission letter
  • Visa application and appointment confirmation
  • Health insurance
  • Accommodation proof
Two timing traps worth writing on the wall

Category and income certificates are usually accepted only for the current financial year — a certificate issued last season is a rejected file.

Credential evaluation for Germany runs on a multi-week turnaround. Started late, it does not delay the visa — it costs the intake.

Annexure I

Open decisions and blockers

Nothing on this list is a build problem. Every one of them is a decision or a piece of information the desk has to supply before more building is worth doing.

Consent for student data

The business is running live with student data now, shares it with partners, and has no separate consent flow. This is the one that carries actual legal exposure, and it applies today rather than at launch.

act now

Grievance officer contact

The cheapest available compliance win: publish a named grievance contact on every property. It costs nothing and is currently missing everywhere.

quick

Lead volume and headcount

How many enquiries a month, and how many counsellors handle them. Every capacity decision in the automation plan is unanswerable without this.

The pattern behind all of it

Across the portfolio the constraint is never capability — it is that thoroughly built things do not get sent, shipped or sold. This project has a deck, a costing, a guide book and a specification, and zero deployed infrastructure. The next useful action is sending something, not building something.
