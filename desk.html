Medical admissions desk sheet — Pune · Mumbai · Bangalore

College by college: what the seat costs, what the hostel costs, whether the hostel exists at all, and what the student will actually pay to live in that specific neighbourhood. Built for the three cities on the College Mantra intake form.

Why this sheet covers these colleges and not fifty thousand.

Scraping every Indian college's own website is not something I can do — and the blocker is not volume or speed, it is that most colleges never publish a machine-readable current fee page at all. Many publish a PDF once a year, some publish nothing and quote on request, and state fee committees revise the numbers mid-cycle. A 50,000-row file would therefore have been mostly generated rather than retrieved, and your counsellors would have quoted invented numbers to families.

So this is the fallback you named yourself: only the colleges College Mantra actually admits into. Your intake form is NEET / medical across Pune, Mumbai and Bangalore, so that is what is built here. Send the College Mantra site link and I will extend this to exactly the college list on it — same format, same depth, including any non-medical courses you place students into.

Where a college does not publish a figure, the row is left blank rather than filled with a guess — as you asked. A blank means "ask the college", not "zero".

Security deposit is typically one to three months' rent in Pune — far lower than Mumbai. Navi Mumbai PGs usually take one to two months. Add brokerage of about one month if an agent is involved, plus an 11-month registered agreement with stamp duty.

Metro effect

Katraj is about to get more expensive

Katraj stayed affordable largely because it had no direct metro. The Purple Line extension south toward Katraj has been approved with an underground station planned. Expect rents to climb the way Swargate's did. Good window for a family renting now — a narrowing one.

One trap specific to this list. DY Patil Navi Mumbai's hostel has no mess facility — hostel charges are quoted excluding food. A family comparing that hostel line against a Pune college whose hostel figure includes mess is comparing two different things. Always ask whether mess is inside or outside the quoted hostel number.
03

How to use this at the counselling table

Lead with the gap, not the college

Between a government seat and a deemed seat in the same city, the difference across the course runs into crores. Show the family that number before discussing college names — it reframes what rank is worth chasing and whether a drop year pays for itself.

Always state the multiplier

Deemed colleges quote per year. Over 4.5 years of tuition plus a year of internship, a ₹25 lakh annual figure becomes well over a crore. Families routinely hear the annual number as the total. Say "per year, times five" out loud.

Quote tuition and living separately

Hostel and mess are billed separately at almost every college here, and several exclude mess from the hostel figure entirely. Give the family two numbers, never one blended one.

Annual escalation is real

DY Patil Pune's fee is documented as rising about 7% each year across the five years. DY Patil Navi Mumbai carries roughly a 2.5% annual hike. Model the loan against the full course figure, not year one.

Blank means ask, not zero

Where a hostel or fee row is blank on this sheet, no published figure was found. Get it from the college in writing before it goes into a family's plan.

Verify against the fee committee

Maharashtra and Karnataka both run fee regulatory committees that can revise private and deemed fees mid-cycle. The committee schedule outranks any listing site, including this sheet.

04

Sources and confidence

Each college card links the page its figures came from. Confidence is highest where an official college domain published the number, lowest where only an aggregator carried it.

Highest confidence — official college domain. DY Patil Pune's MBBS fee structure is published directly on the university's own site (medical.dpu.edu.in) with a downloadable official PDF, including the note that course fees apply for four and a half years with a fifth instalment covering six months.
Lower confidence — aggregator figures that disagree. MS Ramaiah Bangalore is the clearest example on this sheet: one source lists the MBBS course total at ₹1.25–1.3 crore, another answers a fee query with ₹5.75–7 lakh per year excluding hostel, and a third puts management quota at ₹25–35 lakh per year. These are probably describing different quotas rather than contradicting outright — but the sheet shows the spread instead of picking one. Confirm the quota before quoting.
Fee revision is constant. Bharati Vidyapeeth's own listing notes fees are subject to change per institutional policy; Bangalore fees are explicitly subject to revision by the Karnataka Fee Regulatory Committee; DY Patil Navi Mumbai's fee moved by roughly ₹9 lakh across the course between 2022 and now. Nothing on this page should survive into next admission season unchecked.
Two figures that should raise a flag with any family. Bharati Vidyapeeth Pune's total tuition is listed at ₹1,29,80,000 including deposits and exam fees on one source, while another puts total course cost at ₹1.15–1.25 crore all-in and a third at "exceeding ₹1.1 crore". They cluster, which is reassuring, but the spread is over ₹15 lakh. Never quote the midpoint as if it were exact.
