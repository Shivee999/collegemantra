Read this before you quote a number to a parent.

India has 50,000+ colleges. Nobody can honestly hand you verified, current fees for all of them in one build — fees change every admission cycle and vary by category, quota, and state-vs-outside-state. So this tool does not pretend to cover every college in India. It covers real, sourced figures for the major national institutions in each sector — every IIT, every NIT, every IIM, the top NLUs, AIIMS — plus honest typical ranges for the broader state and private tier of each sector, and a clear "not sourced" label wherever I have nothing to stand behind. Every specific college fee you see here was pulled from a live web search while building this, with its source linked.
